Privacy Policy & Data Security

How we protects your privacy through client-side QR generation.

Our Zero Data Retention Approach

This website generates the QR code solely within your browser. We do not save any of your data—such as UPI ID, name, amount, transaction details, or payment status—on our servers. There is no database, backend payment logging, or payment tracking system.

No Backend Server

QR encoding algorithms are executed inside your browser’s JavaScript engine. No payment payload is deliberately sent to our backend.

No Database Storage

We do not host a database, SQL instance, or a cloud store containing our users’ payment information.

No Account / Login

You will not be required to log in or enter phone numbers, OTPs, or email addresses to use our QR generator.

Bank-Grade Security

The money is transferred directly from your UPI app to the bank through an encrypted NPCI channel. We don't have access to any of your money.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect/store/track your UPI ID / VPA.
  • We do not collect/store/track your name, business name, or transaction notes.
  • We do not track/store/collect transaction amounts, dates, or frequencies.
  • We do not store/track/collect payment status or banking credentials.
  • We do not sell or monetize user data with advertising networks.

2. How QR Code Generation Works

When you input your UPI ID and amount, our client-side JavaScript encodes these as a UPI Uniform Resource Identifier string and renders a QR code directly within the browser.

upi://pay?pa=...&am=...&cu=INR

TThe generated QR information is stored in the browser's active memory, while the page is open in user's tab. After page refresh or closure, the generated in-memory state is cleared.

3. Cookies & Local Analytics

We do not use tracking cookies, profiling beacons or any other intrusive analytics scripts. Theme preference might be stored locally in your browser using localStorage so that your preferred Dark or Light Mode is saved between visits.

4. Direct Payment Execution

When a payer scans the generated QR code or clicks an action item that takes him to a supported payment option, his/her smartphone could launch an installed UPI enabled application such as Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm or BHIM which authenticates the transaction through the relevant UPI authentication process and executes the payment through the user's bank and UPI system.

We are not a party to the financial transaction and does not receive, hold, or settle the payment funds.

External Resources

This website may load publicly hosted technical resources such as web fonts or JavaScript libraries from external content delivery networks. Such services may receive common technical connection data necessary to provide the requested resource. We does not send payment transaction data to such services in the course of QR generation.
